OUR ESSENCE

Where Ancient Royalty Found Their Coastal Haven

"To stay at The Grand Miramare is not merely to book a room; it is to step into a preserved golden era of Mediterranean aristocracy."

— Count Antonio della Rovere, Founder (1928)

Conceived by master Italian architects along the precipice of sun-drenched coastal cliffs, The Grand Miramare originally served as a summer residence for European aristocracy. Over nearly a century, the estate has welcomed Nobel laureates, artists, statesmen, and discerning travelers who cherish seclusion, bespoke hospitality, and artisanal grace.

Every stone archway, hand-carved Roman colonnade, and hand-painted ceiling fresco has been meticulously restored. Today, we balance this illustrious legacy with cutting-edge climate consciousness, farm-to-table culinary sanctuaries, and holistic seaside wellness.
